Site information

Privacy

Visitors

You can explore the site without registering. We do not include third-party analytics or advertising.

Business enquiries

Once the contact form is enabled and you confirm submission, we will store the details you provide: name, business, location, category, email or phone, optional description and permission to contact you. These are used to manage and review your enquiry. They are not automatically published and do not subscribe you to advertising.

The form will show the data controller, enquiry channel and retention period before accepting submissions. Submission remains disabled until this information is configured.

Access and operation

Only the authorised administrator can view enquiries. The server uses a necessary session cookie to protect forms and private access. The hosting provider may keep technical logs. Abuse prevention uses identifiers derived from the connection; these are not included in the public directory.

The illustrations are conceptual. The Jericó photograph retains its attribution and licence. Credit links lead to websites with their own policies.

The data controller's details and retention conditions must be completed before accepting enquiries on the hosting server.
