Pilot stage · Starting in Jericó

A portal that grows with every place.

We are starting in Jericó, learning alongside local businesses and gradually expanding to other destinations. Our goal is to help you connect the parts of your trip with useful local information.

Where we are today

You can already explore 21 Antioquia guides in Spanish and English without an account. The business participation pilot will start in Jericó; a published guide does not mean that local businesses have already joined.

Our journey

We are here

01 · Now

Explore and prepare

We are introducing the guides and the portal’s purpose. We are preparing interest registration and request management. The form will indicate when it is ready to receive information.

02 · Short term

Jericó pilot

We will talk with interested businesses, review their information and prepare the first authorised listings. Every listing will be reviewed before publication.

03 · Medium term

Grow step by step

Building on what we learn in Jericó, we will add other Antioquia municipalities according to local participation and available information. Nationwide expansion is part of the portal’s vision.

04 · Long term

Improve with the community

We will assess new tools to manage content, businesses and events based on the real needs of visitors and participants. New features will be announced once their scope is defined.

When will it launch?

The information portal is already available. Dates for opening registration and publishing the first business listings will be announced here once confirmed. These stages describe our direction; they do not yet have committed dates.

How the process will work

Do you have a business and want to take part?

  1. Share your details once the form is enabled.
  2. We review your request and contact you to learn about your business.
  3. We explain the scope and participation terms, including any costs.
  4. We prepare your listing using information and photographs you authorise for publication.
  5. We review the content with you and agree on publication.

Expressing interest does not purchase a service, generate charges or automatically publish your business. Free or paid participation options have not yet been defined; you will receive the terms before deciding.

What this first stage offers

Information to discover destinations and local offerings. You can browse freely; this stage does not include bookings, payments or traveller or business accounts.
