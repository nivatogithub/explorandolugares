How does it work?

  1. Leave your basic business details and a way to contact you.
  2. We review your enquiry and talk with you about your business.
  3. If we move forward, we prepare the information and authorised photos for your listing with you.
  4. Before publishing it, we agree on the details and participation conditions.

Your questions answered

Who can take part?

Businesses, shops and activity or service providers in the 21 municipalities available in the form. You do not need to own a hotel or create an account.

What does this first stage offer?

An information portal for discovering destinations and their local offerings. We collect enquiries and prepare a directory with reviewed information. Registering does not automatically publish your business or guarantee inclusion, visits or sales.

Can I keep using other platforms?

Yes. You can keep your other channels. Here, we want visitors to discover your business in the context of the destination: its history, attractions and services they may need. At this stage, we do not manage bookings or payments.

How much does participation cost?

Plans and prices have not been defined yet. Submitting this form does not purchase a service or authorise charges. Any commercial proposal will be explained and agreed with you before proceeding.

How will the platform grow?

We are currently focusing on information guides and meeting interested businesses. The next step is to expand destinations and reviewed listings. Other features will be considered later; they are not part of today’s offering.
