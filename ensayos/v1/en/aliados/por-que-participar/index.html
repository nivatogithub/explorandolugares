The value of a connected presence

Your business does not exist
apart from the destination.

Explorando Lugares aims to help local businesses present themselves within the context of the destination the visitor wants to explore.

For local businesses

A presence that explains where you are and what you do.

Google Maps, Waze and social media are useful for locating a business, showing reviews, hours, photos or a link of its own. This portal aims to complement those channels: it presents your offering within the destination’s history, attractions and real needs.

  • Context: your business appears alongside the guide to the place the visitor wants to explore.
  • Identity: a listing can explain what your business offers, show authorised photos and lead to your contact channels.
  • Direct contact: people can contact you directly to confirm important details.
  • A clear alternative: the future proposal seeks a presence with transparent terms, without relying exclusively on models that charge for every booking or transaction.

A website of your own may be the best choice for some businesses; it also requires a domain, hosting, design, implementation and updates. Explorando Lugares does not replace an independent website: it can be a simpler entry point connected to the municipality.

What we aim to build

A national portal that grows municipality by municipality, with centralised information for travellers and a contextual showcase for local businesses. Options, costs and any payment method will be explained before they are offered.
