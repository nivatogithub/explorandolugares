What does the star ★ mean?

It identifies a Premium subscription. It is not a review, a traveler rating or a quality certification. Business Page and Premium cards keep the same size; Premium stands out through its star and greater content capacity on its page.

Compare the two dedicated pages

If you operate experiences

If your business has the category Experiences and activities, as its primary or an additional category, you can add operator placement in a published experience in the same place. A paid subscription is required: it cannot be purchased alone or with Free.

The add-on costs COP $29,900 per month for the selected experience. Featured plus this add-on totals COP $79,800 per month. A directory listing does not automatically include this placement in the experience.

From your account to publication

01

Create and verify your account

Register as a person and confirm your email. Manage your businesses from My account.

02

Find or register your business

Claim an existing business or request a new registration. We review your relationship with the business before linking it to your account.

03

Choose your subscription

Free or one of the three paid subscriptions. For paid subscriptions, choose monthly, quarterly, semiannual or annual billing and submit payment information and proof.

04

Complete and publish

Validating payment proof does not automatically publish the business. Its information and images are reviewed before publication is approved.

The total is shown before you submit: a quarter costs the equivalent of 2.5 months; a half year, 5.2; and a year, 10 months.

Subscriptions offer presentation and visibility; they do not guarantee visits, sales or bookings.

Before paying: review and activation

  • We will publish your business within five (5) business days after receiving payment. Saturdays, Sundays and Colombian public holidays are excluded. Before paying, provide the information and reference images required for your subscription.
  • The paid term starts when the subscription is approved and published, not when you transfer money or submit payment proof. Time spent awaiting publication does not consume the purchased term.
  • Before publication we review your relationship with the business, payment, information and applicable images. Submitting proof or confirming payment does not automatically publish the business or activate its benefits.
  • If payment proof is rejected, My account shows the reason and next step. If information or images need correction, publication remains pending and the paid term has not started.

If publication ultimately proves impossible, we will explain why and handle your request for a resolution or refund under the contract and applicable rights.
