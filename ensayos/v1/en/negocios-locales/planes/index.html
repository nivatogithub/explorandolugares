Optional add-on

Appear as an experience operator

Your subscription provides a directory listing. If your business has the category Experiences and activities, as its primary or an additional category, you can also purchase operator placement in a published experience in the same place.

  • Requires Featured, Business Page or Premium. It is not sold separately or with Free.
  • Choose an experience your business actually provides. It must be published and belong to the same place as your business.
  • In addition to your directory listing, your business name appears within the experience with a link to your card or page, depending on your subscription.
  • Placement requires confirmed payment and approved publication. If the experience is unpublished, the advertisement stops appearing.

An additional COP $29,900 per month for the selected experience. For example: Featured ($49,900) + experience ($29,900) = COP $79,800 per month.

The add-on costs COP $74,750 per quarter, $155,480 per half year or $299,000 per year. These amounts are added to your subscription for the same period.

This add-on provides operator visibility; it does not include bookings or guarantee sales or visitors.

From your account to publication

Validating payment proof does not automatically publish the business. Its information and images are reviewed before publication is approved.

How it works →

Before paying: review and activation

  • We will publish your business within five (5) business days after receiving payment. Saturdays, Sundays and Colombian public holidays are excluded. Before paying, provide the information and reference images required for your subscription.
  • The paid term starts when the subscription is approved and published, not when you transfer money or submit payment proof. Time spent awaiting publication does not consume the purchased term.
  • Before publication we review your relationship with the business, payment, information and applicable images. Submitting proof or confirming payment does not automatically publish the business or activate its benefits.
  • If payment proof is rejected, My account shows the reason and next step. If information or images need correction, publication remains pending and the paid term has not started.

If publication ultimately proves impossible, we will explain why and handle your request for a resolution or refund under the contract and applicable rights.
