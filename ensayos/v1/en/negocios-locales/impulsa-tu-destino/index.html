Support your destination

A destination grows with
trusted local promoters.

We want to meet people and businesses with local roots who can help connect real information about each destination.

Local destination promoter

Help activate the community.

A verified person or business that knows the municipality and helps bring businesses, information and opportunities together. The role will be assigned carefully; we seek one main promoter and, when appropriate, one supporting person.

I want to apply as a promoter ↗

Proposed benefits

A way to recognise local work.

As an initial proposal, a validated promoter could receive a 20% discount on their own business plan, a 20% commission on the first month of each business activated with their code and 10% on the remaining monthly payments of the first year. These terms require validation and a written agreement before they apply.

View promoter terms ↗

What we look for

  • Real knowledge of the destination and verifiable local relationships.
  • Interest in sharing useful information for visitors and businesses.
  • Willingness to undergo a validation process before representing the destination.

Applying starts a conversation; it does not assign the role, activate a code or generate discounts or commissions automatically.
