City walls, neighbourhoods and memory by the Caribbean

Cartagena combines urban heritage with Caribbean life. Explore the historic centre, discover its fortifications and make time for Getsemaní, its streets and local economy. Look beyond the façades to understand the city’s history.

## Historia

Cartagena’s colonial founding dates to 1 June 1533. Its Caribbean bay made it a strategic port within the Spanish colonial system. This history includes Indigenous people and enslaved Africans, and extends beyond architecture and trade.

Defences were built and expanded over several centuries. Walls and forts reveal the relationship between the city, its bay and struggles over maritime routes. On 11 November 1811 Cartagena proclaimed its absolute independence from Spain.

In 1984 UNESCO inscribed the port, fortresses and group of monuments. This is living heritage: Centro, San Diego and Getsemaní retain residential, cultural and commercial uses.

## Por qué visitar

Walk among the historic centre’s walls, squares and balconies.

Discover Getsemaní, its neighbourhood life and cultural expressions.

Explore Caribbean history through fortresses and museums.

Combine city and sea with a trip to Barú or the Rosario Islands.

## Ocho atractivos

### 1. Historic centre and city walls

Centro and San Diego are neighbourhoods in Cartagena’s historic core. Explore their walls, squares and streets, from the Clock Tower and Portal de los Dulces to San Diego square and Santo Toribio church.

### 2. San Felipe de Barajas Castle

This fortification overlooks the historic land approach from San Lázaro hill. Check tickets, routes and accessibility conditions with its manager.

### 3. Getsemaní

A historic neighbourhood associated with artisans and working communities. Its streets and contemporary artistic expressions offer another view of Cartagena; ask permission before photographing people.

### 4. Rosario Islands

Caribbean archipelago off Cartagena, with island scenery and protected marine ecosystems. This is an excursion beyond the urban centre: confirm access, navigation conditions, permits and visitor capacity with managers and operators.

### 5. Playa Blanca · Barú

Playa Blanca is on Barú, the peninsula south of Cartagena, outside the urban centre. The beach and the Rosario archipelago are distinct places. Confirm access and services before travelling.

### 6. La Popa hill and convent

Santa Cruz de La Popa convent stands on La Popa hill, combining religious heritage with views over Cartagena. Its cloister offers another way to experience this historic complex. Confirm visiting and transport conditions before going up.

### 7. San Pedro Claver sanctuary and museum

Religious and museum complex linked to the memory of Pedro Claver and enslaved African people in Cartagena. Check visiting conditions with its manager.

### 8. Bocagrande

An urban beachfront district south of the historic centre, with high-rise buildings along the Caribbean coast. Its waterfront, hotels and restaurants reveal another side of Cartagena, distinct from Barú and the Rosario Islands.

## Experiencias

### Walk through Centro and San Diego

Explore squares, balconies and sections of the city walls at your own pace. The Clock Tower and Portal de los Dulces are stops within the historic centre. This is a self-guided visit, without a guide or booking included.

### Visit the History Museum

Explore the History Museum’s exhibitions in the former Palace of the Inquisition for a closer look at Cartagena’s past. Check the programme and admission conditions with the museum.

https://www.muhca.gov.co/

### Explore the city by sightseeing bus

City Sightseeing presents a sightseeing route with stops in different parts of Cartagena. Check the current itinerary, boarding points and conditions directly with the operator.

https://citysightseeing.com.co/es/bus-turistico-cartagena/

### Scuba dive in the Rosario Islands

Diving Planet presents dives and introductory programmes around the Rosario Islands. Ask which plan suits your experience and check its requirements; this listing does not include a booking.

https://divingplanet.org/

### Go snorkelling in Rosario

Explore the marine surroundings from the surface on a snorkelling outing. Diving Planet lists this activity; confirm the route, equipment and conditions before booking.

https://divingplanet.org/

### Sail on a catamaran

https://www.bonavidacatamaranes.com/
