# Geographic places · expanded drafts

## Teyuna · Lost City

Teyuna is an archaeological site in the Sierra Nevada de Santa Marta, within Indigenous territory. Its stone terraces, paths and structures preserve part of the region’s cultural heritage. ICANH administers the park, and visits are subject to access conditions and territorial protocols.

## Playa Blanca

This beach on Santa Marta’s coast lies in a bay framed by hills near El Rodadero. Its sandy shore and coastal setting give it an identity distinct from Rodadero’s urban beach. It is not Playa Blanca on Barú.

## Minca

Minca is a district settlement in the foothills of the Sierra Nevada de Santa Marta. Its mountain landscape combines forests, rivers and coffee growing, with a distinct identity within Santa Marta.
