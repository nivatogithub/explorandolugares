# Experiences · Santa Marta

## Diving from Taganga

Explore Tayrona’s underwater scenery with a dive centre based in Taganga. Choose a trip suited to your experience and check requirements, sea conditions and authorised areas directly with the operator.

## Hike to Cabo San Juan

Combine tropical forest trails with Cabo San Juan’s beaches and rocks. Before booking, check the route, physical demands, authorised access points and the park’s closure calendar.

## Nature and coffee in Minca

Explore Minca’s mountain scenery and coffee culture. Farm visits and trails depend on the chosen itinerary: check what is included, access conditions and physical demands with the operator.

## Guided walk through the historic centre

Discover streets, squares and Santa Marta’s history with a guide. Check the meeting point, tour language and participation arrangements directly with the organiser.

## Trek to Ciudad Perdida · Teyuna

Follow a multi-day trek through the Sierra Nevada to Teyuna Archaeological Park. The journey requires planning, a guide and physical preparation; check access conditions, permits and Indigenous territory protocols with the operator and site authorities.

## Sailing along the coast

See the coast from the sea on a sailing outing. The route depends on the operator’s programme, weather and applicable permissions; confirm the departure harbour, onboard arrangements and itinerary before booking.

## Visit Centro de Vida Marina

Explore the exhibitions at the Marine Life Centre, formerly Acuario Rodadero, and learn about Colombia’s Caribbean biodiversity. Check the programme, access arrangements and visiting conditions directly with the venue. It is a different institution from Acuario Mundo Marino.

## Playa Blanca boat excursion

A boat trip from El Rodadero offers a day on the beach and along this bay on Santa Marta’s coast. Check authorised transport, boarding points and sea conditions before leaving. This excursion visits Playa Blanca in Santa Marta, not its namesake on Barú.

## Personal watercraft outing

A personal watercraft outing should take place with an authorised provider in a permitted area. Before booking, check age requirements, supervision, protective equipment and sea conditions, as well as service availability and restrictions.
