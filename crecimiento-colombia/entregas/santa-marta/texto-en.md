# Santa Marta, between the Caribbean and the Sierra

The Caribbean meets the Sierra Nevada in a district combining a historic centre, beaches and mountains. Explore each area at its own pace: Taganga, El Rodadero, Minca and Tayrona have different landscapes and identities.

## History

The historic centre offers an introduction to the city's colonial heritage. The Cathedral and the Customs House are cultural landmarks, complemented by the Tairona Gold Museum. Its collections help explain regional heritage without portraying Indigenous communities only as figures from the past.

The Quinta de San Pedro Alejandrino preserves another part of this history: Simón Bolívar died there on 17 December 1830. The site combines the historic house, museum spaces and a botanical garden.

## Tayrona National Natural Park

Trails through tropical forest, rock formations and beaches such as Cabo San Juan. The park lies within Santa Marta district; check entry points, closures and authorised areas with National Parks before planning a trip.

## Quinta de San Pedro Alejandrino

The house where Simón Bolívar died in 1830 brings together history, a museum and gardens. This cultural visit within Santa Marta complements a walk through the historic centre.

## Historic centre and Cathedral

Walk through the centre, past the Cathedral and around Parque de los Novios. Architecture and urban life share this route; the Cathedral is a stop rather than a duplicate attraction.

## Taganga

The bay, fishing boats and coastal viewpoints define this rural district of Santa Marta. It deserves its own visit and a future guide distinct from El Rodadero; diving trips are researched separately.

## El Rodadero

An urban beach and waterfront on Gaira Bay, with a character different from downtown and Taganga. This area of Santa Marta has its own tourism identity and serves as a starting point for sea outings.

## Minca

This rural district in the Sierra Nevada foothills offers mountain scenery, coffee and birdwatching. Plan it as a trip from the city while keeping its distinct identity: it is neither a separate municipality nor a Santa Marta beach.

## Tairona Gold Museum · Customs House

The Customs House brings together archaeological heritage and stories about the societies of the Sierra Nevada and Santa Marta. It adds cultural context beyond beaches and colonial history.

## El Rodadero Aquarium and Sea Museum

A visitor venue devoted to marine fauna on the coast near El Rodadero, reached by sea. It is separate from Acuario Mundo Marino. Check access arrangements and the visitor programme directly with the venue.
