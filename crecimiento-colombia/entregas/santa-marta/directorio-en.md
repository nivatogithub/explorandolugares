# Free directory · Santa Marta

## Hotel Boutique Don Pepe

A boutique hotel combining accommodation and colonial architecture in Santa Marta.

## Casa de Leda

A boutique hotel in Santa Marta’s historic centre offering accommodation and wellness facilities.

## Hotel Boutique Casa Carolina

A historic-centre boutique hotel with accommodation, a restaurant and wellness facilities.

## Ouzo

A Grupo Ouzo restaurant around Parque de los Novios. Explore its food offering directly through the venue’s own channels.

## Little Ouzo

A Grupo Ouzo restaurant with pizza, pasta and Mediterranean-inspired cuisine.

## Serena Tropical

Serena Tropical Bistro presents breakfast, lunch and dinner options in Santa Marta. Check menus and arrangements directly with the venue.

## Expotur

An operator of trips to Minca, Tayrona and Ciudad Perdida from Santa Marta. Check itineraries, requirements and availability directly with the company.

## Magic Tour Colombia

An operator of treks to Ciudad Perdida from Santa Marta. Check the route, physical demands and arrangements directly with the company.

## Fundación Museo Bolivariano / Quinta de San Pedro Alejandrino

A cultural institution preserving Quinta de San Pedro Alejandrino and presenting heritage associated with Simón Bolívar and Latin American art.

## Teatro Santa Marta

A heritage venue in the historic centre devoted to performing arts. Check an up-to-date programme before planning to attend a performance.

## Centro de Vida Marina

A Santa Marta institution devoted to exhibitions and education about marine biodiversity, formerly known as Acuario Rodadero.
