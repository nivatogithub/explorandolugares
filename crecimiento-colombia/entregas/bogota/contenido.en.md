# Bogotá: culture, mountains and neighbourhood life

Bogotá brings archaeological collections and contemporary art together with views from its eastern hills. Group a first visit by area: the historic centre and its museums, Monserrate and, on a separate outing, the Botanical Garden. The directory is informational; it does not indicate partnerships or confirmed availability. [S02–S04,S11–S12]

## Historia / History / História

Bogotá’s history predates Spanish arrival: the savanna was Muisca territory. The colonial foundation dates to 1538. The historic centre connects that past with today’s cultural institutions. The city’s story should not be reduced to a single moment, and accounts of the exact founding site should not be presented as settled fact. [S01]

## atractivos

### Gold Museum

Explore Colombia’s archaeological heritage at the Banco de la República museum beside Santander Park. Check guided visits, admission and current programming with the museum. [S02]

### Monserrate

The hill combines a basilica, city views and nature activities. Confirm the available ascent method: the operator’s website displays a funicular-only notice for a September–October interval. [S03]

### José Celestino Mutis Botanical Garden

A place for research, conservation and environmental education focused on regional plants and ecosystems. Check Tropicario admission and programming before visiting. [S04]

### MAMBO

Bogotá’s Museum of Modern Art hosts exhibitions and cultural activities downtown. Check the current exhibition and access from Carrera Sexta. [S11]

### Teatro Colón and the National Arts Centre

Check guided visits and the programme for each venue. Ticket or registration requirements depend on the activity. [S12]
