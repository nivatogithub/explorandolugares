# Bogotá · revisión editorial v3

Bogotá brings together heritage, museums, neighbourhood life and urban nature. Explore the historic centre and La Candelaria, discover its art and archaeology collections, and plan another outing for Usaquén, the parks or the Botanical Garden. Group visits by area to enjoy the capital at your own pace.

## Historia

The Bogotá plateau was Muisca territory before the arrival of the Spanish. The colonial founding dates to 1538; the historic centre’s streets and buildings preserve traces of different stages of a city that continued to grow and change.

The main square took the name Bolívar Square in 1846, following the installation of Pietro Tenerani’s monument to the Liberator. Its surrounding buildings and institutions offer a way to explore the capital’s civic history.

Today, museums, markets, neighbourhoods and cultural venues reveal Bogotá’s diversity. La Candelaria and the Banco de la República collections introduce its heritage, while the urban skyline and eastern hills reveal other scales of the city.

## Ocho atractivos principales

### 1. Bolívar Square

Explore Bogotá’s civic heart, framed by the Primatial Cathedral, National Capitol, Palace of Justice and Liévano Palace. View their façades and the Bolívar monument from the square.

### 2. La Candelaria and Chorro de Quevedo

Walk through the historic centre’s streets of traditional houses, balconies and cultural spaces. Chorro de Quevedo square is a stop to discover the neighbourhood’s atmosphere and stories; explore the surrounding streets at your own pace.

### 3. Gold Museum

Discover goldwork and other archaeological objects that introduce Indigenous societies from the territory of Colombia. The Banco de la República museum stands beside Santander Park; check its official exhibitions and tours.

### 4. Monserrate Hill

The sanctuary in the eastern hills and its viewpoints offer another perspective on Bogotá. Check conditions for the footpath, funicular and cable car before ascending, as operations can change.

### 5. Botero Museum

In a house in La Candelaria, explore works by Fernando Botero and an international art collection. Combine your visit with the Banco de la República Cultural Block’s other venues.

### 6. Usaquén: square, streets and market

In northern Bogotá, stroll around the historic square, traditional houses and dining venues. The crafts market offers another reason to visit; confirm its schedule with the organisers.

### 7. Bogotá Botanical Garden

Discover plant diversity and environmental education at the José Celestino Mutis Botanical Garden. Check conditions for the main garden and the Tropicario, which have their own access arrangements and activities.

### 8. Simón Bolívar Metropolitan Park

Enjoy green spaces, walking paths and lake views in the central park. It offers an outdoor break within the city; other venues in the wider recreational complex are separate visits.
