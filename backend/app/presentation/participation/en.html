Why take part

Your business, within the place visitors want to explore.

Explorando Lugares connects place guides with local businesses. Choose the subscription that fits the information you want to present.

Free shows name and category. Featured adds description and contact. Commercial Page provides a dedicated page and cover. Premium adds story, services, optional sections and up to two additional photos.

Photos and contacts are published according to the purchased subscription and relevant permissions. A listing does not guarantee visits or sales.

Register a new business or claim an existing one from your account. Requesting information is an enquiry and does not register a business.
