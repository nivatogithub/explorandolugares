How to read the figures and explore the data

One person may open several pages and click several times. We do not measure unique people or sessions. Contact clicks do not confirm conversations, bookings or sales.

We store totals by day, place, business, contact channel and connection country. These tables do not store IP addresses, analytics cookies, browser identifiers or individual journeys. We respect browser exclusions and privacy signals. Blocking and undetected automation may affect counts; they are not a traffic audit.

Free listings may be preloaded; they do not imply a purchase. Accounts and subscriptions show the current state.

HOW WE EXPAND THE PLATFORM

Our tourism coverage.
Step by step.

Explore the expansion plan

The initial goal covers 221 municipalities and territorial units selected for their tourism offerings, as well as places with their own identity within them. Other areas are outside the current scope; this does not mean they lack attractions. New discoveries may expand the selection. Jericó and Cauca Viejo have separate guides; sharing a municipality does not link their publications.

We advance in three priority groups. Departments and places have their own classification: priority 1 for the initial launch, priority 2 for the next expansion and priority 3 for later development. We work in batches we can review and maintain.

We prepare each place before publishing. We gather reliable sources, permitted reference photographs, history, attractions and practical information. We add local businesses to the free directory and review text, translations and images.

We publish gradually and keep updating. The map lets you explore available guides. Drafts and internal research are not announced automatically. An available guide does not mean that all attractions and businesses in the municipality are included.

We measure the portal's actual reach. The published-place count comes from public guides. The Visitors section shows views and interactions from the connected measurement source.
